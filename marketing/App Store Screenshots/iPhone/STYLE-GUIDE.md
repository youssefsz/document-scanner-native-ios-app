# iPhone App Store screenshot style

- Canvas: 1320 x 2868 px, portrait, RGB PNG with no alpha channel.
- Campaign background: cobalt-to-indigo generated artwork in `sources/campaign-background.png`.
- Realistic headline: Helvetica Neue Bold, 98 px, centered, white, two short lines.
- Code-rendered headline: Helvetica Neue Bold, 104 px, centered, white, two short lines.
- Supporting copy: Avenir Next Medium, 40 to 42 px, centered, pale blue.
- Header zone: keep the top 600 px for benefit-led copy.
- `Realistic`: preserve the supplied app UI inside the black titanium frame treatment. Keep the slim bezel, physical side controls, and bottom crop consistent.
- `Code Rendered`: preserve the supplied app UI inside the fixed code-rendered shell. Use the same 1060 px screen width, 24 px border, shadow, phone position, and bottom crop on every image.
- Sequence: lead with the broad benefit, then use one feature or outcome per screenshot.
- Naming: two-digit sequence followed by a short feature name, for example `01-library.png`.
