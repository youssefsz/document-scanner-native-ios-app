Common questions

How do I restore DocScanner Pro?

Open the Pro screen and choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the original one-time purchase.

Why can I not scan a document?

Open iOS Settings, find DocScanner, and confirm that Camera access is enabled. Then reopen the app and try again.

Where are my documents stored?

Documents are stored locally inside the app on your device. DocScanner does not provide an account or developer-operated cloud library. Export any files you need before deleting the app or changing devices.

How do secure folders work?

Secure folder names, PDFs, and previews are encrypted on the device. iOS uses Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode to authorize access. DocScanner does not receive your biometric data.

How do password-protected exports work?

Choose password protection when preparing a PDF copy for sharing. The password protects the exported copy, while the document saved in DocScanner stays unchanged. Send the password to the recipient through a separate channel.
